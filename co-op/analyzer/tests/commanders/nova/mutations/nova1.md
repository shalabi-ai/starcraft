# Nova-Specific Notes (P3 Soldier of Fortune)



## The mutators that usually demand a completely different game plan for Nova are:

| Mutator | Nova Threat Level | Description | Nova Approach |

|----------|------------------|-------------|---------------|

| Propagators | Extreme | Self-replicating monsters that instantly convert units and structures they touch into additional propagators. | \*\*P3 strongly recommended.\*\* Prioritize propagators above mission objectives. Use Nova in Shotgun mode, Griffin Airstrike, Spider Mines, Siege Tanks, and Liberators. Never A-move. Keep army spread out and engage from maximum range. |

| Polarity | Extreme | Each enemy is assigned to one player and can only be damaged by that player. | Coordinate constantly with your ally. Focus exclusively on your polarity targets. Use Griffin Airstrike and Tactical Airlift to quickly clear your assigned enemies. Avoid overcommitting where your ally cannot assist. |

| Void Rifts | Extreme | Void Rifts spawn throughout the map and continuously generate enemy units until destroyed. | Open aggressively with Nova hero solo. Use Griffin Airstrike on distant rifts. Prioritize rift hunting over objectives. Tactical Airlift is invaluable for rapid response. Fast map control is essential. |

| Double Edged | Extreme | Units take damage equal to the damage they deal. High burst damage can kill your own units instantly. | Avoid Siege Tank and Liberator-heavy compositions. Rely more on Nova, Ghosts, Marines, Ravens, Airstrikes, and Defensive Drones. Use abilities carefully and avoid overkill. |

| Heroes from the Storm | Very High | Powerful enemy heroes accompany Amon's armies and level up over time. | Save Airstrikes for hero engagements. Use Nova Snipe and Ghost EMPs. Focus heroes first before fighting the supporting army. Prevent heroes from accumulating levels. |

| Kill Bots | Very High | Kill Bots periodically appear and require unit sacrifices or they unleash devastating attacks. | Maintain a reserve of Marines specifically for sacrifices. Avoid expensive low-unit-count compositions without sacrificial units. Keep track of sacrifice requirements before major pushes. |

| Black Death | Very High | A plague spreads among player units, dealing damage over time and disabling healing. | Split your army into smaller groups. Avoid clumping. Favor mechanical units and ranged engagements. Nova should often operate separately from the main force. |

| Transmutation | Very High | Enemy units evolve into stronger units when they get kills. | Minimize losses at all costs. Use Airstrikes proactively. Defensive Drones are critical. Focus dangerous enemies before they can score kills and snowball into stronger forms. |

| Micro Transactions | Very High | Every command issued costs minerals and gas. | Minimize unnecessary micro. Use control groups efficiently. Queue commands when possible. P1 and P3 perform well because they rely heavily on hero abilities and cooldowns rather than constant army control. |

| Just Die! | High | Enemy units revive once after being killed, returning with full health. | Expect every fight to last twice as long. Save cooldowns for the second life when possible. Siege Tanks, Liberators, and sustained DPS compositions perform well. Avoid wasting Airstrikes on enemies that will immediately revive. |



## Conversely, Nova is one of the best commanders against:



* Missile Command
* Aggressive Deployment
* Void Reanimators
* Boom Bots
* Outbreak
* We Move Unseen
* Going Nuclear



because of Griffin Airstrike, Defensive Drones, Tactical Airlift, and her ability to quickly snipe priority targets with low unit count.
